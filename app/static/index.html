Algorithmic Recommendation Cutoffs

STRONG APPLY ≥ 75 pts

High conviction opportunity backed by robust GMP, heavy subscription interest, or solid financial fundamentals.

APPLY 60 – 74 pts

Favourable risk-reward with healthy demand indicators and reasonable valuation metrics.

NEUTRAL 45 – 59 pts

Mixed signals; consider only if retail subscription or GMP accelerates closer to closing.

AVOID < 45 pts

Unfavourable risk-reward due to muted demand, negative/negligible GMP, or elevated valuations.
